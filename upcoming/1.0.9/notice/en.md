HanCutE plans to revise its Terms and Privacy Policy to clarify photo editing features and
personal information processing.

The main changes will:

- Align descriptions of the system photo picker, face covering, image saving, and sharing with the App.
- Clarify the limitations of face covering and the need to check results before saving or sharing.
- Explain processing of inquiries, attachments, and inquiry access tokens, including retention for one year after resolution.
- Explain default analytics/crash-reporting settings, separate OFF controls, preservation of existing OFF choices, and service-specific retention.
- Clarify that the service has no age restriction and revise the children’s privacy section.
- Clarify that users decide on personal or commercial use of completed images while respecting third-party rights, and preserve rights permitted by law and open-source licenses.

Until the effective date, the [current Terms](https://thkim0118.github.io/hancute-legal/terms/en)
and [current Privacy Policy](https://thkim0118.github.io/hancute-legal/privacy/en) remain in effect.
The release date of App version 1.0.9 is separate from the effective date of these revisions.

For questions about these changes or privacy rights, use Contact Us in the App settings or
the operator contact listed in the current Privacy Policy.
