## 1. Service Overview

The App is a mobile application that allows users to combine multiple photos into a single image using selected layouts.

### Key Features
- Select photos with the system photo picker first, then choose a layout and merge
- Crop adjustment (pinch zoom, drag)
- Add, remove, rotate, and reorder photos
- Detect face regions and cover them with blur or emoji; adjust the cover size and position
- Gap, round corner, and background color settings
- Save JPEG/PNG images and share using the operating system share feature
- Inquiry (submission, reply viewing, follow-up conversations), notices, and FAQ

### Service Characteristics
- Photo merging, face detection, and face covering are performed on-device. Photos selected for editing and face detection results are not uploaded to the Operator's servers.
- Images attached to inquiries and inquiry content are transmitted for support. When you share an exported image with another app, the selected service's privacy policy applies.
- Separate from editing photos, information is processed for inquiries, advertising, usage analytics, error/crash diagnostics, and update management. Usage statistics and crash reporting are ON by default and can be turned OFF separately in App Settings. Existing OFF choices are preserved on upgrade. See the [Privacy Policy](https://thkim0118.github.io/hancute-legal/upcoming/1.0.9/privacy/en) for data items, purposes, retention, and collection controls.
