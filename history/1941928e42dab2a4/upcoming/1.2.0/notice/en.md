# Changes to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

> **Public review proposal** — This notice is not final and does not replace current policies. Matters under review and the proposed effective time may change.

- Prepared at: `2026-10-30T16:00:05+09:00`

Hello, and thank you for using HanCutE.

As we prepare HanCutE 1.2.0, we plan to update our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service:

- We will clarify the planned changes to the inquiry service's storage environment and our retention and deletion information. Starting with 1.2.0, we plan to migrate to self-hosted Supabase on an Operator-managed NAS. The final notice will reflect the actual migration and any remaining copies.
- We will clarify the default settings for usage analytics and error reporting and how to change them. A default setting or acknowledgement of a notice is not recorded as separate consent.
- We will align the descriptions of face covering, image export resolution, and advertising with the features offered.

- Target release date for App 1.2.0: **October 31, 2026**, subject to review and actual availability
- Advance notice publication date and time: **The actual publication time will be recorded after publication is verified**

For questions or privacy rights requests, use the in-app inquiry form or contact choir0118@gmail.com.

The NAS transition is being prepared; final synchronization and operational cutover have not yet been completed. The NAS is located in the Republic of Korea, and encrypted inquiry-related backups are stored in Cloudflare R2. The R2 bucket is marked APAC, but the actual storage country is unconfirmed. No automatic deletion deadline for completed backups was found in the checked settings; the retention and deletion policy is under review.
